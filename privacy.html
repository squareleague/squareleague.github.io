Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Square League mobile application (the "App") handles your information. The App is published on Google Play by Square League and on the Apple App Store by Hua Toan Phong (together, "we", "us"). Contact: huaphongit@gmail.com.

In short

  • The App has no account system. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts or location.
  • Your maps, settings and recorded videos are stored on your device only. We do not run servers that receive them.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob and sells a VIP subscription through Google Play / Apple App Store using Unity In-App Purchasing. These third parties collect some device data as described below.
  • The App is intended for users aged 13 and over.

1. Information stored on your device

The following data is created by the App and kept locally on your device. It is not sent to us.

  • Maps and settings: the maps you build, their thumbnails, your preferences, unlocked items, play tickets and ad-free timers.
  • Recorded videos: when you tap Rec, the App renders the match to a video file and saves it to your device's gallery (Android: Movies/Square League; iOS: the Photos library). On Android 8 and 9 the App requests storage permission for this purpose only. On iOS the App requests "add to Photos" permission only; it cannot read your existing photos or videos.
  • Sharing: the Share button opens your operating system's share sheet. What you share, and with whom, is handled by the app you choose.

Uninstalling the App deletes maps and settings. Videos already saved to your gallery remain until you delete them.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any content you create. We do not operate our own analytics or crash-reporting servers.

3. Third-party services

3.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

The App displays app-open, interstitial and rewarded ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects:

  • IP address (used to estimate your approximate location);
  • device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA (subject to your tracking choices) and the App Set ID;
  • ad interactions such as ad views, taps and video completions;
  • diagnostic information such as app launch time and performance data.

This data is shared with Google and, depending on your consent, with Google's advertising partners. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or in a US state with applicable privacy laws, the App shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy Settings item in the App's menu (shown where required). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID and limit ad personalisation in your device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads; iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking / Apple Advertising). VIP subscribers do not see app-open or interstitial ads.

3.2 Unity In-App Purchasing (VIP subscription)

Subscriptions are handled with the Unity IAP SDK (Unity Technologies). According to Unity's disclosure, the SDK collects purchase history, device identifiers, approximate location, crash logs and diagnostic data, and transient account identifiers, for the purpose of delivering purchases and for analytics. This data is not shared with other parties by Unity. See Unity Privacy Policy and Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

3.3 Google Play and Apple App Store (payments)

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing or Apple's App Store. We never receive your card number or billing address. The store provides us only with an anonymous purchase token so the App can unlock VIP. Refunds and payment data are governed by Google's and Apple's privacy policies.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and is listed for ages 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store personal data on our own systems, these rights are mostly exercised through:

  • the Privacy Settings form inside the App (ad consent);
  • your device's advertising settings (reset or delete the advertising ID);
  • Google (My Ad Center) and Unity's privacy tools linked above;
  • deleting the App, which removes all locally stored data.

You can also email us at huaphongit@gmail.com and we will assist you within 30 days. We do not sell personal information.

7. Data retention and security

We retain nothing on our servers because we have none. Third parties retain data according to their own policies. Data sent by the SDKs is transmitted over encrypted connections. Third parties may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States, under their respective legal safeguards.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or applicable laws change. The effective date at the top will be updated, and material changes will be noted in the App's store listing or inside the App.
